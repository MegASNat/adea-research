Terms of Use

Last updated 14 November 2026

These terms explain what you can expect from Adea and what we ask of you when you use this website, join the community, or take part in one of our programs, such as the 30-Day Research Sprint.

Who we are

Adea is a free, student-led project that helps high school students start research, or start again. It was founded by Meg, a high school student, and is run by students and volunteers. This website and everything on it is operated by Adea.

Using this website

You may browse, read and share anything on this site. Please do not:

  • try to access accounts, forms, files or admin areas you have not been given access to;
  • copy or republish our tutorials, program materials, logo or brand as your own;
  • submit work that is not yours, or that you do not have permission to submit;
  • use the site or the community to harass anyone or to send spam.

We may remove content or withdraw access when these terms or our Code of Conduct are not respected.

Membership and programs

Joining Adea is free and open to high school students anywhere. Some programs have their own dates, steps or eligibility, which are stated on the page or form for that program. Where a program is judged, such as the Sprint, decisions are made by our judges and are final.

Adea is run by volunteers, so we may need to change program dates, formats or deadlines. When we do, we update the program page and, where we have your email for that program, we tell you.

Work you submit

You keep ownership of any question, draft, research or other work you submit. By submitting it, you give us permission to read it, share it with the mentors and judges of that program, and give you feedback. We will only feature your work or your name publicly, for example in a shout-out or a list of finishers, if you agree to it.

You confirm that the work you submit is your own. AI tools may help you understand a topic, but they must not write your work for you.

Our materials

Our name, logo, tutorials, templates and program materials belong to Adea, or to the students and volunteers who made them. You are welcome to use them for your own learning and at your school. Please ask us before using them commercially or redistributing them.

Links to other sites

We link to tools and pages we do not control, such as Google Forms, Discord and reading material. We share those links because they are useful, not as an endorsement of everything on them. Those services have their own terms.

Availability and liability

We run this site as a free, volunteer, student-led project and provide it as it is. We cannot guarantee that the site, the community or any program will always be available or free of errors. Adea gives general guidance on how to do research; it is not academic, legal or professional advice, and we are not responsible for decisions you make based on it.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as Adea grows. When we do, we change the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will say so on the site or by email.
