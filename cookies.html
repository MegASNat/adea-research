Cookie Notice

Last updated 14 November 2026

This site keeps as little as possible in your browser. We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell browsing data.

What we store

  • Your cookie notice choice. When you close the notice at the bottom of the page, we save that in your browser’s local storage so we don’t show it again. That is the only thing this site stores.
  • Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google Fonts. To send them, Google receives your browser’s request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Third-party forms and community. Sign-ups use Google Forms and our community lives on Discord. Those pages follow Google’s and Discord’s own cookie practices, not ours.

What we do not do

We do not place advertising cookies, we do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not share browsing information with anyone for marketing.

Managing this yourself

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you clear them, the cookie notice will simply appear again. Everything else on the site keeps working.

Questions about this page?

Write to adearesearch@gmail.com and a member of the team will get back to you.
