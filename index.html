Who we are

More than a study group. A free, student-built community that makes research feel new, not hard, for anyone who wants to start, or start again.

01

Free, always

Every tutorial, mentor session and workspace tool is free. No trials, no premium tier, no paywall between you and your question.

02

Built by students who got stuck too

We've stared at the blank page and the stalled project. Adea is the thing we wished we had: people and structure, not pressure.

03

Community first, software second

You'll never do this alone. The workspace is the quiet part that keeps your project in one place, so your time goes to the real work.

How Adea runs.

Adea is run by students, for students, with volunteer researchers who remember what it felt like to start. Some of these roles are open right now.

Founding team

Sets Adea’s direction, keeps everything free, and makes sure every part of the community stays safe and welcoming.

Community guides

Now recruiting

Students who welcome new members, keep conversations kind, and help people find their next step.

Volunteer mentors

Now recruiting

Researchers who lead short group sessions and leave feedback inside the workspace, never in private DMs.

Tutorial creators

Make the short videos and guides in the free library, one research skill at a time.

Outreach & content

Share Adea with the students who need it most, and tell the stories of people starting their first project.

Ambassadors

Now recruiting
