Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Most of the people who use Adea are high school students, many of them under 18. This page sets out what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and how you can have it removed.

What we collect

  • When you join Adea: your first name, email address, country, school grade, where you are in your research journey, and, if you choose to share them, what you are curious about and how you heard about us.
  • When you apply to the founding team or as a volunteer: the answers on that application.
  • When you take part in a program: what you hand in, such as your research question, sources, plan and draft, and the feedback and results that go with it.
  • When you email us: your email address and what you write.

We never ask for payment details, because everything at Adea is free. We do not ask for home addresses, phone numbers or ID documents.

Why we collect it

To run what you signed up for: to welcome you, send you news about the community, events, mentor sessions and competitions, organise programs, share your work with mentors and judges, issue certificates, and count how many students we reach. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Who can see it

Only the Adea team members who need it for the program or task you are part of. Program submissions are visible to the mentors and judges of that program. Your name and work are only shown publicly if you agree.

We use trusted services to run Adea: Google Forms, Google Sheets and Gmail to collect sign-ups and send emails, Vercel to host this website, and Discord for the community. They process information on our behalf, and each has its own privacy policy. What you post in the Discord community is visible to other members there.

If you are under 18

You are welcome here. We only ask for what we genuinely need, we never publish your contact details, and we do not use personal information for advertising or profiling. One-to-one mentor sessions with students under 18 only happen with a parent or guardian’s consent. A parent or guardian can write to us at any time to see what we hold about their child or to have it deleted.

How long we keep it

We keep member details while you are part of Adea, and program submissions for the program they belong to and our record of results. If you ask us to delete your information, we remove it, except for a minimal record of an award or certificate if one was issued.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information, or to remove your name from anything we have shared.
  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or writing to us.

Security

We keep sign-up data in access-controlled accounts and limit who can see it. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you.

Questions about this page?

Write to adearesearch@gmail.com and a member of the team will get back to you.
