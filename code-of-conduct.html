Code of Conduct

Last updated 14 November 2026

Adea is a place to ask the questions you think are too basic, share work that isn’t finished yet, and help someone take their next small step. This code keeps it friendly and safe. It applies everywhere Adea happens: our Discord, mentor sessions, events, the Sprint, emails and social media.

What we expect from everyone

  • Be kind. Everyone here is learning something new. Welcome newcomers, celebrate small wins, and remember there are no silly questions.
  • Be respectful. People join from many countries, schools and backgrounds. Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working.
  • Give helpful feedback. Talk about the work, not the person. Say what works, then one or two things to try next.
  • Do your own work. Credit your sources and the people who helped you. AI can help you understand, never write for you.
  • Respect privacy. Don’t share anyone’s personal information, messages or work without their permission.
  • Keep it on topic and safe. Use the right channels, no spam, and no content that isn’t appropriate for high school students.

What is not okay

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, insults, hateful or sexual content, threats, pressuring someone into a private conversation, or sharing someone’s information without consent. This applies whether it happens in public or in private messages.

Mentors and volunteers

Mentors and volunteers are here to guide, not to do students’ work for them. Mentor sessions happen in groups, in the open. One-to-one mentor sessions with students under 18 only happen with a parent or guardian’s consent. Mentors and volunteers do not ask students for private contact details or move conversations off Adea’s spaces.

If something feels wrong

Tell us. Message a moderator on Discord or email adearesearch@gmail.com. You won’t get in trouble for reporting, and we will keep your report as private as we can. If anyone is in danger, contact a trusted adult or local emergency services first.

What happens next

We read every report and respond as quickly as we can. Depending on what happened, we may talk with the people involved, give a warning, remove messages, or remove someone from the community or a program.

This code is inspired by community codes such as the Contributor Covenant and those of student-led nonprofits, adapted for Adea.

Questions about this page?

Write to adearesearch@gmail.com and a member of the team will get back to you.
